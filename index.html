Privacy Policy — SingAI: AI Singing Video Maker

Application: com.singingapp
Effective Date: October 11, 2026

Executive Summary: SingAI is a private creative utility. We do NOT harvest, sell, monetize, or transmit your personal photos, videos, or biometrics. SingAI adheres strictly to Google Play's User Data & Photo/Video Permissions Policies.

1. Information We Do Not Collect

SingAI does not require accounts, logins, passwords, credit card numbers, or phone numbers. Specifically:

  • No Biometric Profiling: Facial landmark detection (jawline, eye contours, and mouth shape) is computed locally and transiently strictly for the purpose of lip-sync animation frames. We do not build biometric identity templates or share facial geometries with third parties.
  • No Harvesting of Personal Photos: Selected photos remain strictly under user device control. We do not store your personal photos on remote cloud servers.
  • No Ambient Audio Recording: Microphone access is activated solely when you explicitly tap the record button to capture singing or spoken lyrics for your video.
  • Zero Ads & Zero Data Trackers: SingAI contains zero third-party advertisement networks, analytics trackers, or data brokers.

2. Scoped Permissions & Android Photo Picker

SingAI adopts the principle of least privilege, using Android's modern security architectures:

  • Android Photo Picker (Zero Storage Permissions): SingAI does NOT request broad device storage permissions (READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE or READ_MEDIA_IMAGES). Instead, it uses Android's System Photo Picker (PickVisualMedia), granting temporary scoped read permission solely to the single image you choose.
  • Microphone (Optional): Required only if you choose to record custom vocal audio or spoken lyrics inside the app.
  • Camera (Optional): Used only if you choose to snap a direct selfie for animation.
  • Internet Access: Used for optional cloud neural rendering acceleration and loading curated royalty-free music preview tracks.

3. Data Retention & User Control

Because SingAI does not create or manage user accounts, no identity data is retained in central databases. Videos saved to your device gallery are stored locally on your device storage, where you maintain 100% control to view, export, or delete them at any time.

4. Children's Privacy

SingAI is designed for general audiences and does not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13. If you believe an underage person has provided personal data, please contact our support email for immediate deletion.
