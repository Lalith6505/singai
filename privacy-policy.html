Privacy Policy — SingAI: AI Singing Video Maker

Effective Date: October 11, 2026

Application: SingAI: AI Singing Video Maker (Package: com.singingapp)

Developer: Eng. Lalith Prasanna Madanayake / Sisaara Engineers Private Limited

Summary: SingAI is a standalone creative tool designed to respect your privacy. SingAI does NOT collect, sell, or monetize your photos, videos, biometric data, or personal information. The app complies fully with Google Play's User Data and Photo/Video policies.

1. Information We Do Not Collect

SingAI does not require account registration, login credentials, phone numbers, or credit card information inside the app. Specifically:

  • No Biometric Data Harvesting: Facial landmark analysis (lip, mouth, eye contour detection) is performed solely to compute neural lip-sync frames for your chosen video generation. We do not construct, store, or share facial recognition biometric profiles.
  • No Storage of Photos on Third-Party Servers: Photos you select remain under your device control. Scoped access is granted exclusively through the Android Photo Picker.
  • No Audio Surveillance: Audio recordings made via the built-in microphone recorder are used solely for generating the corresponding singing video. We do not listen to or transcribe ambient conversations.
  • No Advertising Trackers: SingAI is a paid, ad-free application. There are no advertising SDKs, tracking pixels, or data broker integrations.

2. Device Permissions & Scoped Access

SingAI follows the principle of least privilege, utilizing modern Android APIs:

  • Photo Picker (Zero Permission Storage): Rather than requesting broad device storage access (READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE or READ_MEDIA_IMAGES), SingAI utilizes the modern system Android Photo Picker. This guarantees that SingAI only receives a scoped URI to the exact photo you explicitly choose.
  • Microphone (Optional): Required only if you choose to record custom vocal audio or spoken lyrics inside the app.
  • Camera (Optional): Used only if you choose to snap a direct selfie for animation.
  • Internet Access: Used for optional cloud neural rendering acceleration and loading curated royalty-free music preview tracks.

3. Data Retention & Deletion

Because SingAI does not maintain personal user accounts or central databases, there is no persistent storage of your identity. Any generated videos saved to your device gallery remain completely on your local device storage, which you can view, export, or delete at any time via your device gallery.

4. Children’s Privacy

SingAI does not target children under the age of 13, nor do we knowingly collect personal identification information from children. If you believe an underage person has provided personal data, please contact us for immediate review.

5. Changes to This Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy periodically to reflect technological or legal updates. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated Effective Date.
